# Project: GEPAUS​
## Gender Evaluation in Public Austrian Universities with respect to STEM-related fields of study over time.


### Abstract
STEM stands for science, technology, engineering and mathematics. Whereas, this is a much needed field for the development of todays society, there is still a significant gender gap worldwide. On average girls are four times less likely to pursue career in STEM, which is not least due to mental barriers and lack of personal examples. “Research suggests that exposing girls to female inventors at a young age has the potential to reduce the gender gap in technical STEM fields by half.[96] „ Unfortunately the chance of a young girls interacting with women in STEM is much higher in cities as opposed to rural arreas. By exploring the spatial aspect of STEM-related education and employment among girls in Austria we will help detect areas where awareness raising initiatives for STEM are most needed.
